Rung

Get from the top word to the bottom one by changing a single letter at a time. Every rung you stand on has to be a real word — no leaping, no rearranging, one letter per step.

Playing

  • Type a four-letter word and press Enter. It must differ from the rung below it in exactly one position.
  • Backspace on an empty box takes back your last rung.
  • Later ladders have rusted words that will not take your weight. They are listed above the ladder, and the par already accounts for them.
  • Solve it in par to keep your streak. Going long is still a solve.

About the par

The lexicon is 1,786 common four-letter words, hand-picked so that nothing you are asked to walk through is a word you have never met. Two words are neighbours when they differ in one place, which turns the whole lexicon into a graph — 93% of it forms a single connected mass with, on average, seven neighbours per word.

Par is a breadth-first search across that graph, so it is not an estimate or a guess at a good route: it is provably the shortest ladder that exists. If you match it, nobody can do better. If you beat it, something is wrong with my arithmetic.
