Orbital Drift

Your probe has no meaningful engine. Gravity is doing all the work, all the time. The only real decision you get is what to fall toward.

Controls

Hold left mouse
Tether the world nearest your cursor. Its pull on you multiplies while the beam is live. Let go and you keep everything you gained.
Right mouse / Space
Burn toward the cursor. Costs fuel. This is a rescue, not a steering wheel. On a touchscreen there is a burn button in the corner instead.
R
Scuttle the probe and restart the sector
M
Mute

How to fly

  • The dotted line is where you are actually going, tether included. Trust it.
  • Tether on the way in to a world and release at the closest point — that is a slingshot, and it is the fastest thing in the game.
  • Tether on the way out to bleed off speed you cannot survive.
  • Shaving a world at speed builds your multiplier. It decays if you play safe.
  • Beacons have no gravity of their own but pull hard when tethered. Black holes cannot be tethered at all. Stay out of their light.
  • Pulsars fire a ring outward on a fixed beat. Being in the wrong place when the front arrives is a disaster; being in the right one is the cheapest speed in the game. The dotted line knows when it is coming.
  • Wormholes come in pairs. Fall into one and you come out of the other with every bit of your velocity intact.
  • Reach the gate to fold into the next sector. Hitting anything above 120 m/s ends the hull.
