Chrono Echo

Every loop, you record a take. Every take you already made replays beside you as a solid body. Stand on your own history. Make it hold doors open. Get four things done at once with one pair of hands.

Rules of the loop

  • Echoes replay exactly what you did. They are solid — you can stand on them, ride them and be blocked by them. Every take begins where the last one did, so you always start the loop standing inside your own history.
  • Collected orbs stay collected for the whole attempt, across every take.
  • Plates stay down while anything — you or an echo — rests on them.
  • Dying only ends the current take. Your echo dies at that moment too, and the loop rolls on.
  • Run out of takes and the timeline collapses. Start over.
  • Done with the nine? Open the forge and build your own — the whole level packs into a link you can send to anyone.
