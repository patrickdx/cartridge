Cold Spot

Something is in the house. It is in exactly one room. You cannot see it, and you have a limited number of charges to spend on instruments that will each tell you a partial truth. Every reading is honest. None of them is enough on its own.

The instruments

House rules

  • Pick an instrument, then click the room (or corridor) to read.
  • Right-click a room to mark it yourself — ruled out, then suspect, then clear.
  • Charges are the whole budget. Ending a case with charges left is worth points.
  • Turn on assist and the house dims every room your own readings have already eliminated. It halves the score for that case.
  • From the sixth case on, it moves — one room, orthogonally, after every second reading. Old readings still describe where it was. Reason forward.
  • Name the wrong room and the case is lost and the streak with it.
