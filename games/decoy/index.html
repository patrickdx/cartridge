Decoy

You have no weapon. You cannot attack, block, or push. What you have is their attention — and the fact that nothing in this room moves until you do.

How a turn works

  • Nothing happens until you act. Step with the arrow keys or WASD, tap a neighbouring square, or Space to wait. Tapping your own square waits too.
  • Then everything else takes exactly one turn.
  • Anything that is about to be dangerous is drawn before it happens. If a square is marked, do not be standing in it.
  • Clear every enemy in the room to move on. You clear them by standing where their fire has to cross something else.
  • One touch and you lose a life. You get three per run.
