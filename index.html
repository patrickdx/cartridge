CARTRIDGE

A handmade arcade. Five games, no two alike.

About this place

Every game here is built from scratch — no engine, no framework, no asset packs. Graphics are drawn to a canvas at runtime and every sound is synthesised in the browser the moment you hear it. The whole arcade is a few files of plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your progress and high scores live in this browser's local storage and nowhere else.
